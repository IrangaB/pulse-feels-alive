PULSE · FEELS ALIVE — HOW TO UPDATE YOUR WEBSITE
================================================

You can change your website yourself from your admin page. You do not need a developer, and you
do not need any extra software. A web browser on a computer, tablet or phone is enough.

Admin page:  https://<your-domain>/admin
Log in with the username and password you chose during setup.

Every change is live on the website as soon as you press Save.


THE ADMIN SECTIONS
------------------
Categories        The "Product Categories" section on the home page
Gallery           The "Product Gallery" page: photos of finished products with the club name
Fabrics           The "Explore our Fabrics" section
Customers         The "Our Customers" page: the moving logo band and the logo grid
Texts & photos    Banner headline and video, introduction, Our Story text and photo,
                  feature bar image, section headings, Product Gallery and Our Customers
                  page headings
Contact & footer  Contact section text and bullet points, footer text, company details
Enquiries         Every message sent through the website's contact form
Settings          Where enquiry emails are sent, and your username and password


CATEGORIES, GALLERY, FABRICS AND CUSTOMERS
------------------------------------------
These four lists all work the same way. Example: adding a product category.

Add an item
  1. Categories → "+ Add category".
  2. Enter the name, choose an image and enter a display order number.
  3. Tick "Online" to show it on the website straight away, or leave it unticked to save it as
     a Draft.
  4. Press Save.

Online or Draft
  • Online = visible on the website.
  • Draft  = saved in the admin page but NOT visible to visitors.
  In the list, use the "Put online" / "Set to draft" button to switch at any time.
  Use the "Drafts" filter at the top of the list to see all your drafts.

Change an item
  Press "Edit", change what you need and press Save. To keep the current image, leave the image
  field empty.

Change the order
  Lower display order numbers are shown first. Tip: use 10, 20, 30 … so you can later put a new
  item in between (for example 15) without renumbering everything.

Delete an item
  Press "Delete" and confirm. This cannot be undone. If you might need the item again, use
  "Set to draft" instead.

What is different per list
  • Gallery: the name is the club or customer the product was made for. It is shown under the
    photo.
  • Fabrics: also have a description, shown under the fabric name.
  • Customers: the image is the customer's logo. You can also enter their website: then
    clicking the logo opens that website. The logos scroll across the page automatically
    (the band pauses when a visitor points at it) and are also shown in a grid below.


IMAGE TIPS
----------
Categories The website does NOT write the category name or badges (e.g. "Coming Soon") on top
           of the picture. Put them into the image itself, in the same style as the current
           T-Shirts, Neck Warmers and Pipos images. Use square images, about 1000 × 1000 px.
           The name you type is used in the contact form's product list and by search engines.
Gallery    Square photos look best, about 1000 × 1000 px. Photos are cropped to a square, so
           keep the product in the centre.
Customers  Logos as PNG with a transparent or white background, at least 400 px wide.
Fabrics    Upright images, about 800 × 1000 px. The picture is cropped to this shape, so keep
           the important part in the centre.
Banner     Background video: MP4, up to 60 MB. A short loop (10–20 seconds) loads fastest.
All        JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB each.


TEXTS & PHOTOS, CONTACT & FOOTER
--------------------------------
Change any text and press "Save changes" at the bottom.
  • "Heading, red ending" is the part of a heading shown in red (e.g. "Our" + "Story").
  • In longer texts, leave an empty line between paragraphs.
  • Bullet points: one per line.
To replace a photo or the video, choose a new file. Leave the field empty to keep the current one.


ENQUIRIES
---------
Every message from the contact form is:
  • emailed to the address(es) under Settings (dhevapriya@csuomi.fi unless you change it), and
  • listed under Enquiries (new ones are marked "New").
Press the email address to reply. If emails stop arriving, check your spam folder, then use
Settings → "Send a test email".


YOUR LOGIN
----------
Change your username or password under Settings. You need your current password to do this.
After 5 wrong passwords, logging in is blocked for 15 minutes.
You are logged out automatically after 4 hours of inactivity.
Forgot your password? Contact your website developer to reset it. Your content is kept.
